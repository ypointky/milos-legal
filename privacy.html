Milos Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Milos is an app for logging what you eat, your weight and your training, with an AI coach called Milo. It is provided by ACORNAWARE PTE. LTD. (Singapore) (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us when you sign in with them (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email).
  • What you log: meals and their nutrition estimates, meal photos, weight and body measurements, workouts, notes, and what you say or type to Milo (including voice, which is turned into text).
  • Your starting answers: goal, sex, age (year of birth), height, weight, activity level, and any target you set.
  • Apple Health (only if you connect it): weight, height, age and sex, workouts and active/resting energy, steps, sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability. We write back one thing: the weight you log in Milos. We never read medications, cycle tracking, clinical records or any other Health category.
  • Things you share into Milos: photos, links or text you send from other apps with the share sheet.
  • Technical data: a push-notification token and your time zone (to send meal reminders at the right local time), and usage counts of our AI features (for example how many requests were made), used to run and budget the service.

We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store your records, recognize food in photos and text, estimate nutrition, show your trends, and remind you at mealtimes if you ask us to.
  • To let Milo analyze and advise you: Milo reads your records (including Health data you connected) to answer you, write a short nightly note and a weekly review, and calibrate your targets.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it, or share it, for advertising or marketing. Health data from Apple Health is used only to provide the features described above.

3. Where it is stored

Your account and records are stored with our hosting provider Supabase, in its data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Your records are kept in your own account space and are protected so that only you can access them through the app. Data is encrypted in transit.

4. Who helps us (service providers)

  • Supabase — hosting, database, file storage (photos) and sign-in.
  • Google (Gemini API, paid tier) — the AI model Milo runs on. The parts of your records needed for a request (for example a meal photo, what you said, recent days) are sent to it to produce an answer. Under the paid-tier terms, Google does not use this data to train or improve its models. When Milo needs to look something up (for example a restaurant’s dish), the model may run a web search with that query.
  • Google — also delivers our sign-in code emails, and handles “Continue with Google” if you choose it.
  • Apple — “Sign in with Apple”, push notifications, Apple Health (read on your iPhone), and speech recognition for voice input.

These providers process data only on our behalf, to deliver the service. We do not give your data to anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your records for as long as you have an account, so you can see your history. You can delete individual entries in the app. In Settings › Account & data you can erase all records (your account stays and you start fresh) or delete your account (the account and all its data, including photos, are deleted right away). You can also email us at ypointky@gmail.com from your account email and we will do it within 30 days. Disconnecting Apple Health (iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Milos) stops new reads; data already recorded stays in your account until you delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Your records are stored as plain text, so we can give you a complete export. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Singapore’s PDPA) you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

7. Children

Milos is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

ACORNAWARE PTE. LTD., Singapore — ypointky@gmail.com

Milos 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 30 日

Milos 是一款记录饮食、体重和训练的 App，里面有一位 AI 教练 Milo。本 App 由 ACORNAWARE PTE. LTD.（新加坡）（“我们”）提供。本政策说明我们收集什么、为什么收、存在哪里、谁帮我们处理，以及你可以做什么。

1. 我们收集什么

  • 账号：你的邮箱；或你用 Apple、Google 登录时它们给我们的标识（Apple 可能给的是一个转发邮箱，而不是你的真实邮箱）。
  • 你记下的东西：每一餐和它的营养估算、饭的照片、体重和身体测量、训练、备注，以及你对 Milo 说的话和打的字（语音会转成文字）。
  • 开头的回答：目标、性别、年龄（出生年）、身高、体重、活动量，以及你自己定的目标数。
  • 苹果健康（只有你连上了才读）：体重、身高、年龄和性别、训练和活动 / 静息消耗、步数、睡眠、静息心率和心率变异。只写回一样：你在 Milos 里报的体重。用药、生理期、病历等其他健康类别，一概不读。
  • 你分享进来的东西：你从别的 App 用“分享”发进来的照片、链接或文字。
  • 技术数据：推送通知的设备标识和你的时区（为了按你当地的饭点提醒），以及 AI 功能的用量统计（比如请求了多少次），用来运行和核算服务。

我们不用广告标识符，不接任何第三方统计或追踪工具，也不跨 App、跨网站追踪你。

2. 怎么用

  • 提供 App 的功能：存你的记录，从照片和文字里认出吃的、估算营养，给你看走势，你要的话到饭点提醒你。
  • 让 Milo 给你分析和建议：Milo 会读你的记录（包括你连上的健康数据）来回答你、每晚写几句、每周写复盘、校准你的目标。
  • 让服务正常、安全地运行。

我们不卖你的数据，不用来做广告或营销，也不为此给任何人。苹果健康的数据只用于上面说的这些功能。

3. 存在哪里

你的账号和记录存在我们的托管服务商 Supabase，位于德国法兰克福（欧盟）的数据中心。你的记录在你自己的账号空间里，只有你能通过 App 访问。传输全程加密。

4. 谁帮我们处理（服务商）

  • Supabase——托管、数据库、文件存储（照片）和登录。
  • Google（Gemini API 付费版）——Milo 用的 AI 模型。一次请求需要的那部分记录（比如一张饭的照片、你说的话、最近几天）会发给它来得出回答。按付费版条款，Google 不会用这些数据训练或改进模型。Milo 需要查东西时（比如某家店的一道菜），模型可能会用这个问题做一次网页搜索。
  • Google——另外还负责发送登录验证码邮件；你选“用 Google 继续”时也由它处理登录。
  • Apple——“通过 Apple 登录”、推送通知、苹果健康（在你的 iPhone 上读取）、语音输入的语音识别。

这些服务商只代我们处理数据、用来提供服务。除非法律要求，我们不把你的数据给其他任何人。

5. 保存多久，怎么删

只要你的账号还在，我们就保存你的记录，方便你看历史。单条记录可以在 App 里删。在「设置 › 账号与数据」里可以清除所有记录（账号留着，从头开始），或者删除账号（账号和全部数据、包括照片，立即删除）。也可以用你的账号邮箱发邮件到 ypointky@gmail.com，我们会在 30 天内办好。断开苹果健康（iPhone「设置 › 健康 › 数据访问与设备 › Milos」）后不再读取新数据；已经记下的仍在你的账号里，直到你删除。

6. 你的权利

你可以要我们给你一份你的数据、更正或删除它。你的记录是纯文本存的，我们可以完整导出给你。视你所在的地区（比如欧盟 GDPR、新加坡 PDPA），你可能还有更多权利；联系我们，我们会帮你。

7. 未成年人

Milos 不面向 16 岁以下的人，我们不会有意收集他们的数据。

8. 变更

本政策如有实质变更，我们会在生效前在 App 里告诉你。
